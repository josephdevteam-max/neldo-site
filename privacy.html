Privacy Notice

Version 3 · Last updated October 7, 2026 · Draft under legal review. The effective date will be posted here once it is approved.

Where NELDO is today: NELDO is in private testing with a small group of adults through Google Play internal testing and Apple TestFlight. It is not publicly available in app stores. This notice describes the current app and servers, including pantry photo scanning, which is in testing.

The short version

  • No account. You don't need to give us a name, email, or phone number to use NELDO.
  • Your chats, saved meals, plans, and lists stay on your phone.
  • To answer you, NELDO sends your message and relevant settings (like allergies) through our servers to Anthropic's Claude. If Claude can't answer, OpenAI is the backup, then Google Gemini. During private testing, Claude is used only for testers who have agreed to it; the app's permission screen will name Anthropic in the next update. Pantry photos you choose go to OpenAI, with Google Gemini as backup.
  • Our servers don't store your messages or photos. Anthropic and OpenAI may keep what they receive for up to 30 days (longer only if it's flagged for abuse), and Google for a limited time. None of them uses it to train its AI.
  • NELDO doesn't sell your data, show ads, or use your content to train AI models.
  • Turn AI help off or clear NELDO's data on your phone at any time. Email support@neldo.app for anything else.

1. Scope

This Privacy Notice explains how NELDO ("NELDO," "we," "us," or "our"), operated by Neldo LLC in Connecticut, United States, collects, uses, discloses, keeps, and protects information when you use the NELDO mobile app, the neldo.app website, and related services (the "Service"). NELDO is for adults 18 and older in the United States. If you live elsewhere, any rights your local law gives you still apply.

The separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy adds detail about allergy and other health-related information, including rights under Washington, Nevada, and Connecticut law.

2. Information we process

2.1 Information you choose to give us

  • Messages and conversation context. What you type to NELDO, plus up to your last 16 conversation turns so NELDO can follow along, and the meal or cooking step you are working on.
  • Settings. An optional first name or nickname, diet type, allergies, foods to avoid, favorite cuisines, stove or kitchen setup, energy mode, and language.
  • Kitchen items and lists. Ingredients you have, grocery lists, weekly plans, and serving sizes.
  • Pantry photos. Photos of your fridge, pantry, or spices that you take or pick for ingredient scanning (see section 3).
  • Safety reports. If you choose to report an AI response, the excerpt you send (up to 4,000 characters), a category, and optional details (up to 1,000 characters).
  • Support email. Anything you write to support@neldo.app.

Anything you type or show can include personal details, such as a name, a place, or a health condition. Please share only what NELDO needs to help you.

2.2 Information created automatically

  • App and session identifiers. A random ID the app creates on your phone, and a random security session token from our server. Neither contains your name, but they can link requests from the same phone.
  • Device check. Google Play Integrity (Android) or Apple App Attest/DeviceCheck (iPhone), through Google Firebase App Check, confirms that requests come from the real NELDO app. This sends device and app integrity signals to Google or Apple.
  • Connection and request information. IP address, the type of app or browser, request times, and whether a request worked, failed, or was rate-limited.
  • Permission choices. Each request carries your consent version and your adult, AI, and sensitive-information choices, so our servers can respect them.

2.3 What NELDO does not collect

NELDO does not require an account, legal name, email address, phone number, or payment information. It does not read your contacts, use GPS or background location, use an advertising ID, or include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting tools. We don't buy information about you from other companies.

3. Pantry photo scanning

Pantry photo scanning is in testing. When you take or choose a photo (up to 3 per scan, each up to 2 MB), here is what happens:

  • The app sends the photo to NELDO's server only after you have given AI permission. Nothing else about you is attached to a photo scan: no name, allergies, or chat history.
  • Our server checks the file and sends it to OpenAI to list the food ingredients it can see. If OpenAI fails or doesn't answer in time, the same photo goes to Google Gemini instead.
  • Only a list of ingredients comes back to your phone. Our server does not save the photo. It keeps only a one-way fingerprint (a hash) of each request for up to 30 days to stop replay abuse; the photo cannot be rebuilt from it.
  • The app keeps the photo in memory only during the scan. Your original stays in your camera roll or photo library under your phone's settings.
  • AI providers may keep the photo for a time under their own terms (see section 5).

Photo details. Photo files can contain hidden details, such as the date and the place where the photo was taken. NELDO's server removes these hidden details before any AI provider sees the photo, and refuses a photo it can't clean safely. Please still keep people, faces, mail, and documents out of the picture.

4. How we use information

  • Answer your requests: suggest meals, write recipes and cooking steps, scale servings, plan your week, and build grocery lists;
  • Respect your allergies, foods to avoid, and diet choices;
  • Recognize ingredients in pantry photos you choose to scan;
  • Show crisis resources (988 and 911) when a message suggests someone may be in danger;
  • Keep the Service secure: confirm requests come from the real app, prevent abuse and fraud, and control costs;
  • Find and fix errors and measure whether features work, using codes, counts, and timings rather than your content;
  • Review safety reports you choose to send;
  • Answer support and privacy requests, and comply with law.

Neldo LLC reviews safety reports to investigate harmful, offensive, or inaccurate responses and improve safety controls. Reporting is a separate choice and still works if AI help is off. A report is not sent to any AI provider, is not used to train models, and is not an emergency service.

5. AI and cloud service providers

NELDO uses these companies to run the Service:

  • Anthropic (main AI). Through the Claude API, Anthropic receives the information needed for each chat request: your message, recent conversation, relevant settings (which can include an optional name, allergies, and foods to avoid), kitchen items, and the recipe you are working on. Anthropic's commercial terms do not allow it to train models on this content. It deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, except that content flagged for a usage-policy violation can be kept for up to 2 years, and the law can require longer. During private testing, Anthropic receives requests only from testers who have agreed to it. The next app update adds Anthropic to the permission screen, and everyone is asked once before their chats go to Claude.
  • OpenAI (backup AI and pantry photos). If Claude is unavailable or fails, OpenAI receives the same chat request through the OpenAI API. It also reads pantry photos. Each time, it receives the information needed for the request: your message, recent conversation, relevant settings (which can include an optional name, allergies, and foods to avoid), kitchen items, the recipe you are working on, or a pantry photo. We ask OpenAI not to store requests for later use ("store: false"). OpenAI says it does not train its models on API data by default, and it keeps API data for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse. OpenAI also scans images for child sexual abuse material and may keep flagged images longer.
  • Google Gemini (outage backup). If the other providers are unavailable, time out, or return an error, the same request (text or photo) may go to the Google Gemini API. NELDO uses Gemini under Google's paid-service terms: Google does not use these requests to improve its products and keeps them for a limited time only to detect misuse and meet legal requirements.
  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud. Host our servers and security database, run App Check, and keep server logs.
  • Google Play and Apple. Deliver the app and run device checks (Play Integrity, App Attest, DeviceCheck).
  • Google Workspace (Gmail). Handles email sent to support@neldo.app.
  • GitHub Pages and Google Firebase Hosting. Host and redirect the neldo.app website.

These providers process information for NELDO under their business or developer terms. Their own retention and security rules apply to what they receive. Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where they operate. Provider policies: Anthropic data retention, OpenAI API data controls, Gemini API terms.

Safety reports are stored in a restricted Google Cloud database for review by Neldo LLC. The report feature never sends report content to OpenAI or Gemini.

6. When we disclose information

  • To the service providers in section 5, to run the Service;
  • When reasonably necessary to comply with law or legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or security of users, NELDO, or others;
  • As part of a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law and any required notice;
  • When you ask us to or agree to it.

7. Sale, advertising, AI training, and opt-out signals

NELDO does not sell personal data, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising. NELDO has no ads.

NELDO does not collect, use, or sell personal data to train large language models. We don't use your content for training, fine-tuning, or distilling AI models. Under their API terms and NELDO's settings, Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google also do not use NELDO requests to train their models (see section 5).

Where the law requires it, NELDO honors universal opt-out signals such as Global Privacy Control (GPC). The neldo.app website has no ads or analytics.

9. Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information. Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our servers check that requests come from the genuine NELDO app, our security database stores session tokens and IP addresses only as one-way hashes, and direct access to that database is blocked. Access to NELDO's cloud accounts is limited to the company's owners. Data on your phone is protected by your phone's own security, such as its screen lock. No system is perfectly secure.

10. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Choose what to share. You decide what to type, which settings to fill in, and whether to scan a photo. You can add ingredients by hand instead.
  • Turn AI help off in Settings → Privacy & data. This stops new requests to AI providers. It can't pull back what was already sent.
  • Camera and photos. You can deny or remove NELDO's camera or photo access in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Delete on your phone. "Start fresh" clears the conversation. "Clear my NELDO data" removes your conversation, kitchen list, weekly plans, saved meals, favorites, meal history, name, preferences, and session token from your phone. Uninstalling the app also removes them.

Deleting other records. Our servers don't keep your messages or photos, and the security records above expire on their own. To delete a safety report, a support email, or anything else, email support@neldo.app. Include a report case ID if you have one. Because NELDO has no accounts, we may not be able to find records that aren't linked to anything you can give us. If so, we'll explain why and help with the controls on your phone. We can't erase copies held by OpenAI or Google under their retention rules, but we will pass on valid deletion requests where the law requires.

11. U.S. state privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws in states such as Connecticut, California, Washington, and Nevada may give you rights to:

  • confirm whether we process your personal data, and access it;
  • correct inaccurate personal data;
  • delete personal data, subject to legal exceptions;
  • get a portable copy of certain personal data;
  • opt out of sale, targeted advertising, or certain profiling (NELDO does none of these);
  • withdraw consent for sensitive-data processing;
  • appeal if we deny your request;
  • be free from discrimination for using these rights.

To make a request, email support@neldo.app. You don't need an account, legal name, phone number, or ID documents. We may ask for information reasonably needed to find the records you mean. An authorized agent may act for you where the law allows, subject to verification. We respond within the time the law requires (for example, 45 days, extendable once where allowed).

To appeal a decision, email support@neldo.app and say you want to appeal. We'll respond in writing within the legal deadline. You can also contact the Connecticut Attorney General, Washington Attorney General, Nevada Attorney General, California Privacy Protection Agency, or your state regulator.

12. California notice at collection

If you live in California, this section adds to the rest of this Notice. In the last 12 months NELDO may have collected: identifiers (app and session IDs, IP address); internet or other electronic activity (request and error records); audio, electronic, or visual information (pantry photos); sensitive personal information (allergy or health details you share); and other information you provide (messages, preferences, kitchen items). We use them for the purposes in section 4, disclose them to the service providers in section 5, and keep them for the periods in section 8. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we use sensitive personal information only to provide the Service you ask for. 13. Sensitive and health information

NELDO is for everyday planning and general wellness. It can help organize meals around preferences, allergies, or a limit you give it, including an instruction from your healthcare professional. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, decide medical care, or change medication. Nutrition numbers are estimates. NELDO is not a medical device. Ask a healthcare professional for medical advice.

Allergies, health-related food limits, and health details you type or show can be sensitive or consumer health information. NELDO asks for your permission before sending this kind of information to AI providers, and uses it only to help you with what you asked. Saying no doesn't make NELDO quietly ignore an allergy. The Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy explains more.

14. Age and children

NELDO is only for adults 18 and older. The app asks you to confirm your age before use. We don't knowingly offer the Service to, or collect information from, anyone under 18. If you think a minor has given us information, email support@neldo.app.

15. Recipe library (DataWheel)

NELDO includes a curated recipe catalog. Curated means selected and structured for the product, not medically verified. In this release, DataWheel runs in "shadow mode": it may check the structure of a generated meal in server memory and then discards it. Nothing from your conversations is added to a shared library, and shared reuse of generated recipes and feedback-based learning are turned off. Before NELDO ever adds user-derived content to a shared library, we will update this Notice and ask for any consent the law requires.

16. Changes to this Notice

We may update this Notice as NELDO changes or the law changes. We'll post the new version here with its date and, for material changes, tell you in the app before the change applies and ask for any consent the law requires.
