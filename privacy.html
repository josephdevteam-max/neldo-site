Privacy Notice

Version 4 · Effective October 10, 2026

NELDO is for adults 18 and older in the United States, on iPhone and Android.

The short version

  • No account. You don't need to give us a name, email, or phone number to use NELDO.
  • Your chats, saved meals, plans, and lists stay on your phone.
  • To answer you, NELDO sends your message and relevant settings (like foods you never want) through our servers to outside AI companies: a main one that writes the answer, another that checks many messages for safety at the same time, and others as backups. Pantry photos you choose go to one outside AI company, with another as backup. Nothing goes to them until you tap "Accept and start." If your app still shows the older permission screen, update NELDO. You'll be asked once to tap "Accept and start" before new AI requests are sent.
  • Our servers don't store your messages or photos. The outside AI companies may keep what they receive for a limited time to check for abuse, up to 30 days at our main one, longer if it's flagged for abuse or the law requires it. None of them is allowed to use it to train its AI.
  • Crash reports are off unless you turn them on in Settings → Privacy & AI. If you do, they go to Google's Firebase Crashlytics and never include your chats.
  • NELDO doesn't sell your data, show ads, or use your content to train AI models.
  • Turn AI help off or clear NELDO's data on your phone at any time. Email support@neldo.app for anything else.

1. Scope

This Privacy Notice explains how NELDO ("NELDO," "we," "us," or "our"), operated by Neldo LLC in Connecticut, United States, collects, uses, discloses, keeps, and protects information when you use the NELDO mobile app, the neldo.app website, and related services (the "Service"). NELDO is for adults 18 and older in the United States. If you live elsewhere, any rights your local law gives you still apply.

The separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy adds detail about health-related information you choose to share, including rights under Washington, Nevada, and Connecticut law.

2. Information we process

2.1 Information you choose to give us

  • Messages and conversation context. What you type to NELDO, plus up to your last 16 conversation turns so NELDO can follow along, and the meal or cooking step you are working on.
  • Settings. An optional first name or nickname, how you eat (an eating style like vegetarian, halal or kosher, and a No pork pick), goals you set for a meal plan (like calories), foods you never want (Never include), favorite cuisines, stove or kitchen setup, energy mode, and language. NELDO doesn't ask about allergies.
  • Kitchen items and lists. Ingredients you have, grocery lists, weekly plans, and serving sizes.
  • Recipe votes. If you rate a recipe from NELDO's recipe library, the app sends only that recipe's ID and your vote.
  • Pantry photos. Photos of your fridge, pantry, or spices that you take or pick for ingredient scanning (see section 3).
  • Safety reports. If you choose to report an AI response, the excerpt you send (up to 4,000 characters), a category, and optional details (up to 1,000 characters).
  • Support email. Anything you write to support@neldo.app.

Anything you type or show can include personal details, such as a name, a place, or a health condition. Please share only what NELDO needs to help you.

2.2 Information created automatically

  • App and session identifiers. A random ID the app creates on your phone, and a random security session token from our server. Neither contains your name, but they can link requests from the same phone.
  • Device check. Google Play Integrity (Android) or Apple App Attest/DeviceCheck (iPhone), through Google Firebase App Check, confirms that requests come from the real NELDO app. This sends device and app integrity signals to Google or Apple.
  • Connection and request information. IP address, the type of app or browser, request times, and whether a request worked, failed, or was rate-limited.
  • Permission choices. Each request carries your consent version and your "Accept and start" choice, which includes your 18+ confirmation, so our servers can respect it.
  • Crash reports (only if you turn them on). "Send crash reports" in Settings → Privacy & AI starts off. If you turn it on, and AI help is also on, the app sends a report to Google's Firebase Crashlytics when it crashes or hits an error. A report holds the error type, where in the app it broke, app and phone details (like the phone model and OS version), and a random install ID. It never includes your chats, messages, photos, or name.

2.3 What NELDO does not collect

NELDO does not require an account, legal name, email address, phone number, or payment information. It does not read your contacts, use GPS or background location, use an advertising ID, or include advertising tools or tools that track how you use the app. It sends crash reports only if you turn them on (see section 2.2). We don't buy information about you from other companies.

3. Pantry photo scanning

Pantry photo scanning is in testing. When you take or choose a photo (up to 3 per scan, each up to 2 MB), here is what happens:

  • The app sends the photo to NELDO's server only after you have tapped "Accept and start." Nothing else about you is attached to a photo scan: no name or chat history.
  • Our server checks the file and sends it to an outside AI company to list the food ingredients it can see. If that company fails or doesn't answer in time, the same photo goes to a backup outside AI company instead.
  • Only a list of ingredients comes back to your phone. Our server does not save the photo. It keeps only a one-way fingerprint (a hash) of each request for up to 30 days to stop replay abuse; the photo cannot be rebuilt from it.
  • The app keeps the photo in memory only during the scan. Your original stays in your camera roll or photo library under your phone's settings.
  • The outside AI companies may keep the photo for a time under their own terms (see section 5).

Photo details. Photo files can contain hidden details, such as the date and the place where the photo was taken. NELDO's server removes these hidden details before any outside AI company sees the photo, and refuses a photo it can't clean safely. Please still keep people, faces, mail, and documents out of the picture.

4. How we use information

  • Answer your requests: suggest meals, write recipes and cooking steps, scale servings, plan your week, and build grocery lists;
  • Serve a recipe from NELDO's own recipe library when one fits your request (see section 15);
  • Leave out foods you never want and follow your diet and goals (NELDO can't check food allergies or cross-contact, so check every ingredient and label yourself);
  • Recognize ingredients in pantry photos you choose to scan;
  • Show crisis resources (988 and 911) when a message suggests someone may be in danger;
  • Show 911 right away when a message says someone may be having an overdose, a severe allergic reaction, a heart attack or a stroke right now. Your phone does this itself, without asking AI, even with AI help off or no connection. It can miss a message, so in an emergency call 911 yourself. That message isn't sent at that moment. It stays in your chat on your phone, so if you keep chatting it can go along with your recent conversation. "Start fresh" clears it;
  • Keep the Service secure: confirm requests come from the real app, prevent abuse and fraud, and control costs;
  • Find and fix errors and measure whether features work, using codes, counts, and timings rather than your content;
  • Fix crashes and keep the app stable, using crash reports only if you turn them on;
  • Review safety reports you choose to send;
  • Answer support and privacy requests, and comply with law.

Neldo LLC reviews safety reports to investigate harmful, offensive, or inaccurate responses and improve safety controls. Reporting is a separate choice and still works if AI help is off. A report is not sent to any outside AI company, is not used to train models, and is not an emergency service.

5. AI and cloud service providers

NELDO uses these companies to run the Service:

  • Outside AI companies (chat and pantry photos). Outside AI companies write NELDO's replies and read pantry photos. Each chat request goes to a main outside AI company, with others as backups if it is unavailable, times out, returns an error, or its answer doesn't pass NELDO's checks. On many chat turns, a second outside AI company also reads your message and recent conversation at the same time, only to check for safety, such as signs that someone may be in crisis, and to gauge your energy and tone so the reply fits. Pantry photos go to one outside AI company, with another as backup. Each time, the company receives only the information needed for the request: your message, recent conversation, relevant settings (which can include an optional name, how you eat, goals, and foods you never want), kitchen items, the recipe you are working on, or a pantry photo. Nothing is sent to them until you tap "Accept and start." If your app still shows the older permission screen, update NELDO. You'll be asked once to tap "Accept and start" before new AI requests are sent. NELDO uses them under paid business or API terms. Under those terms and our settings, they aren't allowed to train their models on NELDO requests, and we ask them not to store requests for later use where that setting exists. They keep requests only for a limited time to check for abuse and safety: up to 30 days at our main AI company, and for a limited time under each backup's own terms, which can be longer than 30 days. Content flagged for abuse can be kept longer: up to 2 years at our main AI company, and for the time set by each backup's own terms. The law can require longer. One of them also scans images for child sexual abuse material and may keep flagged images longer. NELDO also uses its main outside AI company to write the recipes in NELDO's recipe library. Those requests hold only recipe outlines, like a cuisine and a meal, and nothing about any user.
  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud. Host our servers and security database, run App Check, and keep server logs.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics. Receives crash reports, only if you turn on "Send crash reports." Google processes them for NELDO as a service provider to help us fix crashes. Crash reports aren't used for ads, aren't sold, and aren't linked to a name or email, because NELDO has no accounts.
  • Google Play and Apple. Deliver the app and run device checks (Play Integrity, App Attest, DeviceCheck).
  • Google Workspace (Gmail). Handles email sent to support@neldo.app.
  • GitHub Pages and Google Firebase Hosting. Host and redirect the neldo.app website.

These providers process information for NELDO under their business or developer terms. Their own retention and security rules apply to what they receive. Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where they operate. The list of outside AI companies NELDO uses is available on request at support@neldo.app.

Safety reports are stored in a restricted Google Cloud database for review by Neldo LLC. The report feature never sends report content to any outside AI company.

6. When we disclose information

  • When reasonably necessary to comply with law or legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or security of users, NELDO, or others;

7. Sale, advertising, AI training, and opt-out signals

NELDO does not sell personal data, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising. NELDO has no ads.

NELDO does not collect, use, or sell personal data to train large language models. We don't use your content for training, fine-tuning, or distilling AI models. Under their business or API terms and our settings, the outside AI companies aren't allowed to train their models on it either (see section 5). NELDO's recipe library is not used to train AI models either.

Where the law requires it, NELDO honors universal opt-out signals such as Global Privacy Control (GPC). The neldo.app website has no ads or analytics.

9. Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information. Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our servers check that requests come from the genuine NELDO app, our security database stores session tokens and IP addresses only as one-way hashes, and direct access to that database is blocked. Access to NELDO's cloud accounts is limited to the company's owners. Data on your phone is protected by your phone's own security, such as its screen lock. No system is perfectly secure.

10. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Choose what to share. You decide what to type, which settings to fill in, and whether to scan a photo. You can add ingredients by hand instead.
  • Turn AI help off in Settings → Privacy & AI. This stops new requests to outside AI companies. It can't pull back what was already sent. NELDO needs AI to work, so chat and new meals stop while it's off. Your saved meals and lists stay on your phone.
  • Crash reports. They're off unless you turn on "Send crash reports" in Settings → Privacy & AI. Turning it off stops new reports and deletes any not yet sent. Turning AI help off stops them too.
  • Camera and photos. You can deny or remove NELDO's camera or photo access in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Delete on your phone. "Start fresh" clears the conversation. "Clear my NELDO data" removes your conversation, kitchen list, weekly plans, saved meals, favorites, meal history, name, preferences, and session token from your phone. Uninstalling the app also removes them.

Deleting other records. Our servers don't keep your messages or photos, and the security records above expire on their own. To delete a safety report, a support email, or anything else, email support@neldo.app. Include a report case ID if you have one. Because NELDO has no accounts, we may not be able to find records that aren't linked to anything you can give us. If so, we'll explain why and help with the controls on your phone. We can't erase copies held by the outside AI companies under their retention rules, but we will pass on valid deletion requests where the law requires.

11. U.S. state privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws in states such as Connecticut, California, Washington, and Nevada may give you rights to:

  • confirm whether we process your personal data, and access it;
  • correct inaccurate personal data;
  • delete personal data, subject to legal exceptions;
  • get a portable copy of certain personal data;
  • opt out of sale, targeted advertising, or certain profiling (NELDO does none of these);
  • withdraw consent for sensitive-data processing;
  • appeal if we deny your request;
  • be free from discrimination for using these rights.

To make a request, email support@neldo.app. You don't need an account, legal name, phone number, or ID documents. We may ask for information reasonably needed to find the records you mean. An authorized agent may act for you where the law allows, subject to verification. We respond within the time the law requires (for example, 45 days, extendable once where allowed).

To appeal a decision, email support@neldo.app and say you want to appeal. We'll respond in writing within the legal deadline. You can also contact the Connecticut Attorney General, Washington Attorney General, Nevada Attorney General, California Privacy Protection Agency, or your state regulator.

13. Sensitive and health information

NELDO is for everyday planning and general wellness. It can help organize meals around preferences, foods you never want, or a limit you give it, including an instruction from your healthcare professional. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, decide medical care, or change medication. Nutrition numbers are estimates. NELDO is not a medical device. Ask a healthcare professional for medical advice.

Health-related food limits and health details you type or show can be sensitive or consumer health information. Tapping "Accept and start" gives NELDO your permission to send this kind of information to outside AI companies, and NELDO uses it only to help you with what you asked. NELDO doesn't ask about allergies and can't check food allergies or cross-contact. Foods you list under Never include are left out, but you must check every ingredient and label yourself. If you mention an allergy in a chat, NELDO leaves that food out and tells you it can't check for allergies or cross-contact. That is not allergy protection. The Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy explains more.

15. Recipe library (DataWheel)

NELDO has its own recipe library. NELDO makes these recipes with AI, starting from a simple outline, like a Mexican dinner for a low-energy night. Nothing anyone types to NELDO goes into them. Every recipe goes through NELDO's recipe and food-safety checks before it's used. The checks are automatic. The recipes are in English, Spanish and Portuguese.

When a library recipe fits what you asked for, NELDO can use it instead of writing a new one. That's faster, and it helps keep NELDO free. NELDO's AI still talks with you. If you ask for something the library can't fully match, like cooking with what's in your fridge, a dish with specific ingredients, a change to a meal, or a limit from your doctor, the AI writes a meal just for you. That meal stays yours. It never goes into the library.

What's not in the library: your messages, your name, your photos, your settings, anything about your health, your saved meals, or the meals made for you.

What NELDO counts: how often each recipe is served, and which kinds of meals the library doesn't have yet (by cuisine, meal and energy, each month). These are plain totals. They aren't linked to you or your phone.

Votes: when you rate a library recipe, the app sends only the recipe and your vote, and NELDO adds one to that recipe's total. Your phone remembers which recipes you rated, so you rate each one once. If you report a library recipe as wrong or dislike it, NELDO's server also keeps a short one-way tag made from part of your network address, never the address itself, with that recipe's totals. A dislike also keeps one with the totals for that kind of meal. A recipe's tags are cleared when NELDO reviews the recipe. These tags only help NELDO tell whether votes and reports come from different places, and they can't point to you. Recipes people don't like, or report as wrong, come out of rotation for review. They aren't deleted.

Leaving foods out: if a recipe has something you never include, NELDO drops it if it's optional, uses its no-pork or vegetarian version, picks another recipe, or has the AI write one without it. It always tells you what changed. Check labels on store-bought items.

Allergies: the library has no allergy information. NELDO can't check for allergies or cross-contact.

Training: the library is never used to train AI. Neither is anything you type.

Before NELDO ever adds user-derived content to the library, we will update this Notice and ask for any consent the law requires.
