About NELDO

What NELDO is, and who makes it.

NELDO is a free AI meal planner and cooking app from Neldo LLC. Chat with it like you’d text a friend: tell it what you’re craving, what’s in your kitchen, your budget and how much time you’ve got. You get meal ideas, a grocery list and step by step help while you cook.

What NELDO does

  • Name the dish, get the real thing.

    Ask for pernil, birria or jollof and you get that dish, not a generic version.

  • Cook with what you have.

    Tell it what’s in your kitchen, or snap a photo of your pantry (in beta) and get a list of the ingredients it sees.

  • Swaps that keep the dish.

    No beans, no cilantro, make it vegan. Same dish, tweaked your way.

  • Fits your day.

    Share your budget and time, pick an energy mode (Lowkey, Everyday or Locked In), or just start talking.

  • Plan 1 to 7 days.

    Meal prep on purpose, plus one grocery list grouped by store aisle.

  • Protein and calorie estimates.

    Every meal shows them, always labeled as estimates. Ask for a target and NELDO aims around it.

  • Leaves out foods you never want.

    Tell it what you never want to eat and it leaves it out. NELDO can’t check food allergies or cross-contact, so if you have one, check every ingredient and label yourself.

  • Cook with me.

    Step by step, with timers right where you need them. In English, Spanish or Portuguese.

Who makes NELDO

Neldo LLC makes and runs NELDO.

The company was founded by Joseph Cruz. He’s Puerto Rican, a disabled veteran and a former cybersecurity professional, and he’s building NELDO to be practical meal help that works with the time, energy and budget people actually have. Read his story.

How to get NELDO

NELDO is in a private beta for adults 18+ in the U.S., on iPhone and Android. It’s not in the public app stores. It’s free, with no account and no subscription, and it speaks English, Spanish and Portuguese. Spots are limited.

How NELDO uses AI

Once you allow AI help, NELDO sends your message, your recent chat and the settings it needs, like foods you never want, to outside AI companies to write the answer. They may keep requests for a limited time to watch for abuse. Your chats, settings and saved meals are stored on your phone, not on our server, and NELDO doesn’t sell your data, show ads or use your content to train AI models. Want to know which outside AI companies we use? Email support@neldo.app and we’ll send you the list. Your data, explained has the details.

NELDO can make mistakes and doesn’t give medical advice. Always double-check ingredients, labels and allergens. Read the food-safety note.
