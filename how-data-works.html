How NELDO handles your information

Last updated October 7, 2026 · Draft under legal review.

Where NELDO is today: NELDO is in private testing with a small group of adults through Google Play internal testing and Apple TestFlight. It is not publicly available in app stores.

A short, plain guide. The Privacy Notice and Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy have the full details and your rights.

Do I need an account or my name?

No. NELDO has no accounts. A name is optional; if you add one, it stays on your phone and goes along with chat requests so NELDO can greet you. Please remember that anything you type or show, like a name or a place, can identify you, and that the app uses a random ID to keep your requests working together.

What stays on my phone?

Your conversation (the last 60 messages), saved meals and favorites, weekly plans, grocery and kitchen lists, settings, and permission choices. NELDO doesn't sync them to a server. On Android, NELDO turns off app-data backup; on iPhone, your own device backup may include them.

What leaves my phone, and who gets it?

Only after you give AI permission. When you ask for something, the app sends your message, your recent conversation, relevant settings (like your name, allergies, and foods to avoid), kitchen items, and the recipe you're on to NELDO's server, which passes it to Anthropic's Claude to write the answer. If Claude is down or fails, OpenAI is the backup, and Google Gemini after that. For now, Claude is used only for testers who agreed to it; the next app update asks everyone once. NELDO's server doesn't save your messages; it keeps only security records without content. Anthropic and OpenAI keep requests for up to 30 days, mainly to watch for abuse, and Google keeps backup requests for a limited time. None of them uses them to improve its AI.

What happens to a pantry photo?

Photo scanning is in testing. You take or pick up to 3 photos. Each one goes, by itself, to NELDO's server and then to OpenAI (or Google Gemini as backup), which lists the food it sees. Only that ingredient list comes back. NELDO doesn't save the photo; the AI provider may keep it for a time under its terms. Your original stays in your photo library. NELDO's server removes hidden photo details, like where a photo was taken, before the AI sees it. Keep people and documents out of the shot, or type your ingredients instead.

Does NELDO train AI with my content?

No. NELDO doesn't use your content to train, fine-tune, or distill AI models, and it doesn't sell your data or show ads. Under their API terms and NELDO's settings, Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google don't use NELDO's requests to train their AI either.

Can NELDO make medical decisions?

No. NELDO is for everyday planning and general wellness. It can work around an allergy or a limit you give it, including one from your doctor, but it doesn't diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent conditions or change medication. Nutrition numbers are estimates. If a message suggests someone may be in danger, NELDO shows 988 and 911. Common crisis phrases are caught on your phone itself, even without a connection.

How do I delete things or stop?

In the app: "Start fresh" clears the conversation, "Clear my NELDO data" erases everything NELDO keeps on your phone, and Settings → Privacy & data lets you turn AI help off. You can also remove camera or photo access in your phone's settings. For anything else, like a safety report (keep its case ID), email support@neldo.app. Requests already sent to AI providers can't be pulled back.

What is the recipe library?

NELDO includes a curated recipe catalog. In this release, nothing from your conversations is added to it, and shared learning from feedback is turned off. If that ever changes, we'll update our notices and ask first where the law requires.
