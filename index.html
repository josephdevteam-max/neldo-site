Energy Modes

Different days. Same NELDO.

Energy isn’t a personality type. It’s what you’ve got right now. Pick a mode, or just start talking.

Lowkey

“I don’t have much in me.”

Always the easier, quicker take. Fewer steps, less cleanup, and it still tastes good.

Like: Honey-garlic salmon rice bowl ~20 min

Everyday

“Just a normal kind of day.”

A familiar, satisfying meal that fits the time and ingredients you have.

Like: Birria-style beef tacos

Locked In

“I’ve got energy. Let’s cook.”

Take your time. Go for the big dish or try a new technique, just because you want to.

Like: Pernil with arroz con gandules ~3½ h

No mode is better than another, and you never have to pick one. Foods you never want, your time and the dish you asked for still come first.

Food culture, for real

Name the dish. Get the real thing.

Ask for pernil and you get pernil, not a “pork bowl.” Puerto Rican food is first-class in NELDO, right alongside gumbo, birria, jollof, phở, ramen, moqueca and a whole lot more. If NELDO can’t match a dish exactly, it’s built to tell you straight and offer something close.

Plan 1 to 7 days · meal prep · groceries

One good meal is great. A whole week is better.

Say “meals for 3 days” or “I need 4 meals.” NELDO fills Your week, shows today’s meal right in chat, and builds one grocery list. It’s grouped by store aisle, with the same items combined.

  • 1 to 7 days. Fill the whole week or just the days that are still open. Change one day without losing the rest.
  • Meal prep, on purpose. Repeats are a feature, not a mistake. Cook once, eat twice.
  • One list, by aisle. Produce, Meat & seafood, Dairy & eggs, Bakery, Pantry, Spices & oils, Frozen. Check off as you shop; “Already have” stays separate.
  • Servings that scale. Change servings and the amounts and grocery list update with it.

Food allergies and special diets

Helpful suggestions. Always double-check.

NELDO can make mistakes. It can’t check food allergies or cross-contact, and it can’t promise a meal fits your dietary needs.

Check every ingredient, product label, substitution, and possible allergen cross-contact before preparing or eating food. NELDO cannot verify how food is made or handled, and a recipe or dietary label does not certify that food is safe for you.

You can tell NELDO foods you never want, and it leaves them out. That’s a preference, not an allergy check. If you have a food allergy, check every ingredient and label yourself, every time, and look over saved recipes again when your needs change.

Dietary preferences are not medical advice. Follow your clinician’s guidance for food allergies and medically required diets. If you are unsure whether a food is safe for you, do not eat it until you have verified it.

I grew up in Section 8 housing, and I went from being homeless to owning my own home. I’m proud of how far I’ve come. I haven’t forgotten what it felt like to need help, or the people who helped me move forward.

Those experiences are a big part of why I’m building NELDO. I’m creating practical meal help that works with the time, energy and money people actually have.

Helping someone feed their family on a hard day matters to me, because I know how much those moments mean when you’re struggling.

Good to know

Questions? Straight answers.

Is NELDO really free?

Yes. Core meal help is free, with no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start without giving your name, email or phone number. NELDO is for adults 18+ in the U.S., and asks you to confirm that the first time you open it.

How do I get it? iPhone or Android?

Both. NELDO is in a private beta on iPhone and Android for adults 18+ in the U.S. It’s not in the public app stores. Email support@neldo.app and tell us which phone you have. Spots are limited, and we’ll reply with how to join.

Who makes NELDO?

NELDO is made by Neldo LLC, founded by Joseph Cruz. Read his story. Questions or feedback? Email support@neldo.app.

What languages does it speak?

English, Spanish and Portuguese, both in the app and on this website. Slang and typos are fine.

How accurate are the calories and protein?

They’re AI estimates, and they’re labeled that way. They’re never 100% exact. If you ask for a target like “60 g protein,” NELDO aims around it. Check product labels and portions when the numbers matter to you.

Does it handle food allergies?

No. NELDO can’t check food allergies or cross-contact. You can tell it foods you never want, in settings or in chat, and it leaves them out. If you have a food allergy, check every ingredient and label yourself and follow your clinician’s advice. Read the food-safety note.

What happens to my kitchen photo?

Scan your kitchen is in beta. Once you allow AI help, NELDO sends the photo to an outside AI company to list the ingredients. Hidden photo details, like where it was taken, are removed first. NELDO doesn’t save the photo, but that company may keep it for a time under its own terms. Want to know which outside AI companies we use? Email support@neldo.app and we’ll send you the list. Your data, explained has the details.

Can NELDO plan my whole week?

Yes, anywhere from 1 to 7 days. Ask for “meals for 3 days” or “4 meals,” repeat dishes on purpose for meal prep, and get one grocery list for the week, grouped by aisle.

Can NELDO give medical or dietary advice?

NELDO helps with meal ideas and cooking based on the preferences you share. It does not replace a dietitian or clinician. Follow professional guidance for food allergies and medically required diets.

NELDO is not a therapist, clinician, crisis service or emergency service. If you need urgent support in the U.S. or its territories, call 988 or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. For immediate danger, call 911. Elsewhere, contact your local emergency or crisis service. More about 988.
