Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Version 4 · Last updated October 9, 2026 · Draft under legal review. The effective date will be posted here once it is approved.

Where NELDO is today: NELDO is in private testing with a small group of adults through Google Play internal testing and Apple TestFlight. It is not publicly available in app stores. This policy describes the current app and servers, including pantry photo scanning, which is in testing.

NELDO helps with everyday tasks, starting with meals. It is not a medical service. Still, health-related details you choose to share can be "consumer health data" under some state laws. This policy explains what that information is, what we do with it, who receives it, and your rights. It adds to our Privacy Notice.

Scope and operator

NELDO is for everyday planning and general wellness. It can help organize meals around preferences, foods you never want, or a limit you give it, including an instruction from your healthcare professional. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, decide medical care, or change medication. Nutrition numbers are estimates. NELDO is not a medical device. Ask a healthcare professional for medical advice.

NELDO is operated by Neldo LLC in Connecticut, United States. Contact: support@neldo.app. This policy covers consumer health data under laws including Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada's consumer health data law (SB 370), and the Connecticut Data Privacy Act. NELDO is for adults 18 and older in the United States.

What counts as health data depends on the law and the situation. A favorite cuisine, a grocery item, or a budget is not health data by itself. A peanut allergy, a doctor's sodium limit, or a pregnancy mentioned in a message can be. A session or device identifier can link information to a person even without a name.

Categories and sources

Depending on what you choose to share, NELDO may process these kinds of consumer health data:

  • Diets, goals, or health info you choose to type, and foods you never want that you enter in Settings or mention in a chat. NELDO doesn't ask about allergies, but an allergy or intolerance you mention can still be health data.
  • Health conditions, symptoms, treatments, or health-related food limits that you type in a message (for example, "my doctor said low sodium"). NELDO doesn't ask for medical records.
  • Pantry photos you choose to scan, which could show medicines, supplements, or special foods.
  • AI replies that reflect your health information, such as a recipe changed for a food limit you gave.
  • Safety reports and support emails that include health details you choose to add.

Sources: you, the photos you choose, settings stored on your phone that the app includes with a request, and the AI replies made for your request. We don't get medical records from healthcare providers, connect to health apps or wearables, buy health data, or use location tracking. NELDO does not use geofencing around healthcare facilities.

Purposes and permission

We use consumer health data only to provide what you ask for: answering your request, leaving foods you never want out of your meals (NELDO can't check food allergies or cross-contact, so check every ingredient and label yourself), recognizing ingredients in a pantry photo, continuing your cooking conversation, reviewing a safety report you send, answering your support email, and keeping the Service secure.

Before the app sends any request to outside AI companies, it asks you once, separately from accepting the Terms, to tap "Accept AI." That one step confirms that you are 18 or older, allows NELDO to send your requests and chosen photos to outside AI companies, and allows NELDO to use health or other sensitive information you choose to share only to help you. NELDO needs AI to work. You can withdraw this permission in Settings → Privacy & data at any time.

We do not use consumer health data for advertising, marketing profiles, model training, or a shared recipe library. If we ever want to use it for a new purpose, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires.

Who receives it

NELDO does not sell consumer health data. We disclose it only to these service providers, to deliver the Service you request:

  • Outside AI companies: a main outside AI company writes replies, with others as backups, and one outside AI company reads pantry photos, with another as backup. They receive the health details included in your request or photo.
  • Cloud and security providers: Google (Firebase and Google Cloud) runs our servers and security checks. Google Play and Apple provide device checks that confirm requests come from the real app.
  • Email provider: Google Workspace (Gmail) handles email you send to support@neldo.app.

The outside AI companies work for NELDO under paid business or API terms. Under those terms, they may not train their models on NELDO's content, and they keep requests only for a limited time to check for abuse.

Safety reports are stored in a restricted Google Cloud database for review by Neldo LLC and are never sent to outside AI companies. NELDO has no affiliates that receive consumer health data. We do not let third parties collect health data through NELDO for advertising or tracking.

Retention and deletion

NELDO's servers do not store your messages, settings, or photos; they pass them to the outside AI companies for your request and keep only security records without content (see the retention table). Settings, chats, and saved meals stay on your phone until you edit or clear them. The outside AI companies keep requests only for a limited time to check for abuse, up to 30 days at our main AI company and longer only if flagged. Safety reports are deleted after 30 days, and backups of our security database are deleted within 98 days.

Requests and withdrawal

Keep your case ID if you send a safety report and later want to ask about it or delete it.

Email support@neldo.app to confirm whether we process your consumer health data, get a copy, get a list of everyone we shared it with, correct it, delete it, or withdraw consent. Please don't send medical records or ID documents. You don't need an account, legal name, phone number, or bank connection. We may ask for reasonable details to find the records you mean. Because NELDO has no accounts and keeps most data only on your phone, we may not be able to link some records to you; if so, we'll explain and help you use the controls in the app.

In the app, "Turn off AI help" stops new requests to outside AI companies, and "Clear my NELDO data" deletes your information from your phone. Turning off AI help doesn't pull back information already sent. Deletion is a separate request we handle under the law, including telling our service providers where required.

To appeal a decision, email the same address and say you want to appeal. We'll reply in writing. Using your rights will never lead to unfair treatment.

Washington rights

If Washington's My Health My Data Act applies to you, you can confirm whether we collect, share, or sell your consumer health data; access it, including a list of all third parties and affiliates we shared it with and how to contact them; withdraw consent; and ask us to delete it. We respond within 45 days of receiving your request, with one 45-day extension if needed and explained. Appeals get a written decision within 45 days. You may complain to the Washington Attorney General.

When we delete data, we also notify the processors and third parties that received it. Copies in backups are deleted on their normal schedule, which for NELDO is within 98 days and never more than six months after we verify your request.

Nevada rights

Nevada consumers can ask us to confirm whether we collect, share, or sell consumer health data; list the third parties we share it with; stop collecting, sharing, or selling it; and delete it. We respond within 45 days of verifying your request, with one 45-day extension if needed and explained. We delete covered data and notify recipients within 30 days of verifying a deletion request. Appeals get a written response within 45 days. You may contact the Nevada Attorney General.

Connecticut and other states

Under the Connecticut Data Privacy Act and similar state laws, consumer health data is sensitive data. We process it only with your consent and only to provide the Service you ask for, and we never sell it. Your Connecticut rights are described in section 11 of our Privacy Notice. You may contact the Connecticut Attorney General.

Changes and contact

We will post updates here with a new date and keep earlier versions available on request. Before we collect a new kind of health data, share it with a new kind of recipient, or use it for a new purpose, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires.

Questions, requests, appeals, and accessibility help: support@neldo.app. Operator: Neldo LLC, Connecticut, United States. Website: www.neldo.app.
