Privacy and deleting your data

Your chats, saved meals, plans and lists stay on your phone. In the app, “Clear my NELDO data” removes them, and so does uninstalling the app. You can also turn AI help off in Settings, under Privacy & data.

To delete anything else, like a support email, or to make a privacy request, email us. How deleting your data works.

In an emergency

NELDO is not a therapist, clinician, crisis service or emergency service. If you need urgent support in the U.S. or its territories, call 988 or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. For immediate danger, call 911. Elsewhere, contact your local emergency or crisis service. More about 988.
